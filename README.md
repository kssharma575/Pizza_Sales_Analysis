🍕 ***Pizza Sales Analysis***

📌 **Project Overview**

This project analyzes pizza sales data for the year 2015 to understand sales performance, customer ordering patterns, and product performance.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that can help management understand revenue trends, 
identify popular pizza categories and sizes, and make better decisions about menu planning, promotions, and operations.

**🎯 Business Problem**

The business wants to answer the following questions:
1. What are the total revenue, total orders, and total pizzas sold?
2. What is the average order value?
3. Which pizza category generates the highest revenue?
4. Which pizza sizes contribute most to revenue?
5. Which pizzas are the best-sellers and worst-sellers?
6. Which weekdays and time periods receive the most orders?
7. What actions could help improve sales performance?

**📊 Dataset Overview**

* Total Records: 48,620
* Total Columns: 14
* Date Range: January 1, 2015 – December 31, 2015
* Unique Orders: 21,350
* Pizza Varieties: 32
* Total Pizzas Sold: 49,574
* Total Revenue: Approximately 817,860.05

**🛠️ Tools Used**

* Microsoft Excel
* SQL

**📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

* Total Revenue: Sum of recorded sales revenue.
* Total Orders: Count of distinct order IDs.
* Total Pizzas Sold: Sum of pizza quantities.
* Average Order Value: Total Revenue / Total Orders.
* Average Pizzas per Order: Total Pizzas Sold / Total Orders.
* Category Revenue Share: Category Revenue / Total Revenue × 100.
* Best and Worst Sellers: Pizza performance ranked by quantity sold.

**🔍 Analysis Performed**

* Calculated key sales performance indicators.
* Analyzed order trends by weekday.
* Evaluated revenue contribution by pizza category.
* Compared sales performance across pizza sizes.
* Identified best-selling and worst-selling pizzas by quantity.
* Reviewed sales patterns to identify potential business opportunities.

**💡 Business Value**

This analysis helps pizza business:

* Understand which products contribute most to sales.
* Plan staffing around busy ordering periods.
* Evaluate menu and promotional opportunities.
* Improve inventory planning using demand patterns.
* Make more informed decisions using sales data.
